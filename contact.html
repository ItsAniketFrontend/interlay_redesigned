— Common questions

A few things people often ask.

For a 3 BHK apartment, expect 14–18 weeks from concept to handover. A villa or large home is usually 24–36 weeks. We share a detailed week-by-week schedule after the brief, and update it weekly during execution.

Yes — we currently work across Rajasthan, Delhi NCR and parts of Gujarat. For projects further out, we'll usually fly in once a fortnight and partner with a vetted local execution team.

Two formats — a fixed design fee, or a percentage of the project budget. Both are agreed in writing before any drawings begin. There are no hidden margins, no kickbacks from vendors. You see every invoice we see.
