— How we work

Six steps.
One studio.

A measured rhythm from first conversation to final styling — designed to keep the work calm and the outcome remarkable.

01

Discovery

An unhurried conversation about how you live, what you collect, and the spaces that have moved you.

02

Concept & Mood

Material boards, references and the architectural intent — translated into a clear visual language.

03

3D Visualisation

Walk through every room before construction begins. We refine in pixels — not on site.

04

Detailing

Drawings, joinery, finishes and BOQ — the unsexy spine that holds an honest project together.

05

Execution

One project manager, vetted contractors, weekly site reviews and a calm WhatsApp thread.

06

Styling & Handover

Art, objects, soft furnishings — the final 5% that makes a house feel like yours.
